About the Project

Understanding the purpose, workflow, and scope of this demonstration project.

This project demonstrates how AI, environmental data, and GIS technology can be combined to assess flood risk and support early warning systems for disaster management. By analysing parameters such as rainfall, river water levels, terrain elevation, and flood depth, the system can categorise areas into different risk levels and visualise them on an interactive map.

Rainfall intensity directly affects surface runoff and river discharge. Terrain elevation determines natural water flow and accumulation zones. River water levels indicate upstream conditions and overflow risk. Flood depth estimates help assess potential impact on communities and infrastructure.

Implemented in This Demo

Flask backend with REST API, interactive dashboard, GIS flood risk map, rule-based risk assessment with API integration, Random Forest model training script, data processing pipeline, analytics charts, alert centre, image gallery, and responsive navigation.

Requires Real Implementation

Verified historical flood datasets, live environmental data feeds from sensors and APIs, integration with official weather and disaster management systems, real-time alert delivery to emergency services, and model retraining with production data.
